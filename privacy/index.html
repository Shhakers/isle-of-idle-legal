Privacy Policy

Isle of Idle · Last updated 19 September 2026

Home Terms of Use

This policy is for the mobile game Isle of Idle (Android package com.isleofidle.app), published by ProtoLudi. It covers Google Play now, and the App Store when that listing exists. The same developer name should appear on the store listing.

Questions: protoludidev@gmail.com

Who this is for

Cartoon fantasy combat. You can be knocked Down. There is no blood, no realistic gore, and no sexual content. Content is aimed at a PEGI 7 / IARC 7+ style rating, same neighbourhood as IdleOn. We market Isle of Idle to teens and adults (13+). It is not a children's app, and it is not a social network.

We do not show ads. We do not sell player data. Notifications stay off until you turn them on. There is no account server in this build.

What we collect today

Progress lives on the device you play on (the app's local storage). We do not run a login, and we do not upload your save to our servers.

That local save can include:

  • the display name you type (up to 16 characters)
  • gold, items, gear, skills, quests, and other game progress
  • settings such as sound, vibration, and whether Notifications are on

If you never export the save, that record stays on the phone. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Account → Delete save removes it from the device. Uninstall wipes it. Android will not restore it from a Google backup.

What we do not collect today

  • an account, or an email we require to play. If you write to support, that inbox holds the address and whatever you attached
  • precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • advertising IDs, or any ad network data
  • crash reports or usage analytics from us (no Firebase, Sentry, or similar in the app)
  • payment card numbers (there is no paid shop yet)

Google Play and, later, Apple run their own stores. Install, rating, and purchase data they hold sits under their policies, not this page.

Notifications

Notifications default to off. If you turn them on, the operating system will ask permission. They are local reminders on this device, not remote push from our server. They can fire when you go Down, when you stand after a death, when food runs out, when a job stops because the pack is full or materials ran out, when the 8 hour AFK cap hits, when a shop boost ends, and when a SPATE, hunt, or crypt you left running needs you.

Turn them off in Settings whenever you want. That does not delete your save.

Export and import

Settings → Account lets you export a text backup and share it through whatever target you pick (Files, email, another app). We do not upload that file. If you send it to someone else, you are the one sharing it. Import replaces the save on that phone.

Children

We do not market this app as a children's product. We do not try to collect personal information from children for accounts, ads, or profiling. The only name we keep is the one typed into the game, stored on the device. Parents and carers can delete the save or uninstall the app. Use Google Play family controls (and Apple's, when the iOS app exists) if you want to lock purchases.

If the isle trips, a crash log stays on this phone. We do not upload it. You can share it from the crash screen or Settings → Account. Mail support exports a save, shares that log if one exists, then opens mail. If you send those files, they sit in the support inbox. We cannot restore a save we never received.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, write to the support address and we will delete that mail from the inbox we control.

Isle Pass and real money (not live)

Isle Pass and other real-money purchases are not for sale yet. There are no ads, and there will not be ads.

When a paid Pass exists, this is the deal we intend to keep:

  • it is convenience and cosmetics: extra AFK time, a second gather line, pack chores, bank space, dyes, titles, extra daily SPATE windows
  • it is not XP skips, death skips, armour, weapons, or combat power
  • checkout goes through Google Play Billing on Android, and through Apple on iOS. We do not see your full card number
  • restore purchases will follow the store account, not a login we invent

We will update this page before charged items go live, including anything the stores require us to say about payment data.

Accounts, cloud, party, and chat (not live)

There is no cloud login today. Local save is the record. If we add optional sign-in, cloud backup, party, or chat, we will change this policy first. Until that update, we are not collecting server-side profiles, friend codes, or messages.

Sharing

We do not sell player data. We do not share the local save with advertisers.

The only sharing that can happen today is:

  • you export a save and pick a share target
  • the operating system and the app store, for install and (later) payments
  • you tap Mail support or Share crash log, and send a file to the support address

How long we keep it

On-device save: until you delete it, reset it, or uninstall. Uninstall is final on this build. Android Auto Backup is off, so Google will not bring the save back. Support email: ordinary inbox retention so we can answer you, then we can delete the thread if you ask.

Your choices

  • Notifications on or off in Settings
  • Export, import, reset, or delete the save in Settings → Account
  • Uninstall the app
  • Email us if you want a copy of anything you sent to the support inbox

There is no account to delete today, because there is no account. Delete save on the device is the wipe. Uninstall is also a wipe. Android will not restore the save from a Google backup. Export first if you care about the run.

Changes

If the game starts sending data off the device, this page will say so, with a new date at the top. Old copies of the APK do not secretly grow a cloud.
